How validation works

Every plain query is answered twice: once through the inverted index, and once by scanning the raw articles with whole-word matching. The second is the oracle. Precision and recall between them measure what preprocessing did — recall above the oracle is morphological matching, not a bug.

Boolean vs free text

Capitalised AND, OR, NOT (or brackets) mean strict Boolean: every term is mandatory. Anything else is read as free text, and stop words are dropped before the search. When an AND finds nothing you get a term-by-term diagnosis and the closest partial matches.
